Products

Proven systems in daily clinical use, designed alongside the healthcare professionals who use them.

SmartDerm

Dermatology eTriage. GPs photograph a lesion and attach it to the referral, and a consultant reviews it remotely. Rolled out to all 360 GP practices in Northern Ireland. The original rollout halved the red-flag waiting list.

smartdermni.com →

ECAT

Measures and evidences service performance for District Nursing, Health Visiting and Children's Nursing. Supports safe working caseloads and workforce planning (Right Nurse, Right Task, Right Time).

discoverecat.com →

Sentinel

Real-time antimicrobial stewardship and infection control, aligned with NICE. Covers C.DIFF/MRSA, hand hygiene and DDD dashboards, with outbreak and AMR alerts. Fed live from PAS, LIMS and pharmacy systems.

Book a demo →

Medicines Optimisation Suite

epics records clinical interventions, allergies and incidents. WriteMed handles medicines reconciliation and the GP discharge letter. Sentinel adds lab data. The suite was designed with hospital pharmacists.

LCID

A full Community Information System with a rich patient record. It covers referrals, caseloads, appointments and clinical notes. It served 5,000+ users across three HSC trusts for over 20 years.

Case Load Allocation

Distributes daily appointments across District Nursing and AHP staff, matching each client's needs to staff skills. Nurses record visit outcomes in a mobile app.

NI Interpreters

Schedules interpreter appointments across HSCNI. Handles 50,000+ requests a year from a pool of 300 interpreters, with mobile check-in and automatic invoicing.

eBrokerage

Speeds up discharge for medically fit patients who need domiciliary care. Needs assessments go straight to care providers through a cloud supplier portal.

Yarra CareConnect

A secure cloud platform that brings data from BLE medical devices, such as glucose monitors and BP cuffs, into one holistic patient view for clinical research.

About

Yarra Software Ltd is a Northern Ireland based software development company, whose primary objective is the delivery of quality software solutions and related support services.

The core of our philosophy is a fundamental belief that our customers and employees are the foundation of our business.

Commitment to Expertise

The team at Yarra Software are a dedicated group of IT professionals, offering a breadth and depth of technical expertise across a wide range of software development products. Yarra Software develops solutions using the best technologies available to meet the demands of customer’s business requirements. Our staff have extensive experience at all levels of project delivery and support. All team members are highly customer focussed, ensuring they deliver an uncompromising service at all times.

Commitment to People

Yarra Software recognise our staff as our primary asset. We aim to reward staff commitment by providing a workplace that offers challenges and benefits second to none. We provide extensive staff training and accreditation programs in job-related tools, methodologies and technologies. This ensures the team is equipped to provide the best services and solutions to our customers, whatever the task at hand.

Commitment to the Customer

For us, the key to all our successful relationships is partnership. These partnerships are built up over a period of time and involve us gaining a better understanding of your business. By developing strong relationships we can best advise you on solutions and work with you to achieve your business objectives. Whatever the size and type of project, we maintain close contact for its duration and beyond, to ensure customer requirements are always the highest priority.

Commitment to Quality

Yarra Software has invested a considerable amount of time and effort into optimising our development processes. In addition to industry best practice, we have developed a suite of custom development tools. These allow us to quickly generate system components based on re-usable code objects and the data requirements of the customer. This reduces costs, increases quality and encourages standardisation of user interfaces.
